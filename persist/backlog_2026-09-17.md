## 2. Forensic Findings & Core Issues Identified

### 🚨 Issue 1: Fixed-Rupee Sizing Flaw & Price Inversion Distortion
* **Symptom:** High-value stocks with huge winning moves generated tiny gross profits, while low-value stocks took disproportionately large share quantities.
  - **MARUTI (SELL @ ₹12,393.79):** The bot had a massive **+42.61 point winning move**, but the sizer allocated only **3 shares**. Gross profit was only **+₹90.14**, but total fees were **₹107.90**, turning a stellar trade into a **−₹17.76 net loss**.
  - **MOTHERSON (SELL @ ₹162.39):** Stock moved in favor (+0.34 pts), producing **+₹26.66 gross**, but fees of **₹105.96** turned it into a **−₹79.30 net loss**.
  - **IRFC (SELL @ ₹78.66):** Sizer allocated **508 shares**. A tiny 15-paise adverse fluctuation resulted in −₹76.20 gross and **−₹137.93 net loss**.
* **Root Cause:** `PositionSizer` currently calculates:
  $$\text{Target Position Value} = \text{Capital} \times \text{Kelly Fraction} \approx ₹35,000–₹40,000$$
  $$\text{Quantity} = \frac{\text{Target Value}}{\text{Entry Price}}$$
  This divides a fixed rupee amount by share price rather than sizing based on **monetary risk per share ($|\text{Entry} - \text{SL}|$)**.
* **Fix Required:**
  - Transition to **Dynamic Risk-Based Sizing (Fixed R-Value)**:
    $$\text{Quantity} = \min\left(\frac{\text{Capital} \times \text{Risk-per-Trade \% (e.g. 0.25\% to 0.5\%)}}{|\text{Entry} - \text{Stop Loss}|}, \text{Margin Cap}\right)$$
  - Dynamically scale the Risk % based on **Favourable Factors**:
    1. **Strategy Quality Score:** Historical Profit Factor & Win Rate from `trades_ledger`.
    2. **Signal Confidence Tier:** Higher sizing on $\ge 0.85$ confidence setups.
    3. **Regime Alignment:** Expansion when strategy regime matches current market regime.
    4. **Risk-Reward Multiplier:** Higher budget on setups with $R:R \ge 2.0$.

### 🚨 Issue 2: Transaction Fee Friction & Partial Booking Penalty on Micro-Quantities
* **Symptom:** Partial profit booking on tiny positions destroys net profitability.

  - Every order costs flat ₹20 brokerage + GST + exchange turnover ($\approx ₹25–₹35$).
  - Total fees paid on MARUTI were **₹107.90**, meaning **₹36 per share was lost to broker fees alone**.
* **Fix Required:**
  - **Micro-Quantity Exemption:** If position quantity is $< 10$ shares (or estimated tranche profit $< 3\times$ order fee), **disable intermediate partial booking orders**.
  - Let micro-positions trail using a single dynamic Stop-Loss / runner exit without triggering extra execution fees.

### 🚨 Issue 9: Mandatory Hard Minimum Entry Prerequisite: 2.0×–3.0× Round-Trip Fee Coverage
* **Clarified Principle:** Strategy targets will vary based on technical factors (ATR, R:R, pivots, swing highs/lows, runner trailing). However, **as an absolute pre-entry gate / economic viability filter**, the bot must NEVER take any trade unless the minimum expected target profit at the sized quantity clears at least **2.0× to 3.0× of that trade's round-trip transaction costs**:
  $$\text{Economic Viability Floor: } \text{Expected Target Reward} \ge 2.5\times \text{Round-Trip Fees}$$
  $$\text{Example: If estimated round-trip fees are ₹60, the target move MUST yield at least ₹150–₹180 gross profit.}$$
* **Behavior:** If a setup has a tight target or small position size where total gross reward at target is barely ₹30–₹60, it is economically unviable and **immediately rejected at entry**, preventing fee bleed.
* **Architecture Implementation:**
  - Enforce in **Gate `G19_MinMove`** and **`G17_CostPrecheck`** as a hard pre-entry check (`g19_mode: enforce`, `min_move_fee_multiple: 2.5`).
  - Wire into the dynamic position sizer: ensure the calculated quantity and minimum price move satisfy this viability floor before generating an opportunity.
* **Target Files:** `backend/risk/gates/g19_min_move.py`, `backend/risk/gates/g17_cost_precheck.py`, `backend/risk/position_sizer.py`, `backend/config/defaults.yaml`.

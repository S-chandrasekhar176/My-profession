## 2. Issues, Blockers & Post-Market Action Items
* **Issue #1 - OptionChainRecorder Overnight Supervisor Deadlock:**
  - **Location:** `ultrabot-web/backend/app.py:408-416` in `_supervise_option_recorder()`.
  - **Why/Mechanism:** During overnight market closed hours, `consecutive_failures` exceeded 10. In `app.py`, `rec._fast_task = asyncio.create_task(rec._fast_poll_loop())` is strictly inside the `else:` branch of `if consecutive_failures > 10: await asyncio.sleep(300.0)`. As a result, once `consecutive_failures > 10`, the supervisor sleeps every 300s but never executes the `else:` block to respawn `_fast_task` when morning market opens.
  - **Post-Market Fix:** Remove `else:` guard so that after the backoff sleep (or when `market_hours.is_market_open()` becomes true), `consecutive_failures` resets to 0 and `rec._fast_task` is respawned.

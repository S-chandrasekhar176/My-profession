### 5. Monday Fyers History Window Defect (`_HISTORY_WINDOW_DAYS = 2`)
- **Severity**: High (Blocks setup qualification until 10:55 IST every Monday)
- **Location**: `ultrabot-web/backend/feeds/fyers_candles.py:L45`
- **Observation**: Scan telemetry across all 20 symbols reports `Insufficient candles (X/20)` until ~10:55 AM IST on Mondays.
- **Root Cause**: `_HISTORY_WINDOW_DAYS = 2` sets `range_from = today - 2 days`. On Monday, `today - 2 days` is Saturday (non-trading). Consequently, Friday's trading data is excluded, leaving only today's live bars starting from 09:15 IST. Because 20 5-minute bars take 100 minutes to form, no symbol can meet the 20-candle minimum until 10:55 IST.
- **Post-Market Action**:
  1. Update `_HISTORY_WINDOW_DAYS = 5` in `feeds/fyers_candles.py` (matching the file's own comment: `# 5 calendar days covers holidays comfortably`).
  2. With 5 days, Monday mornings will pull Thursday and Friday's bars (240+ candles), enabling immediate strategy evaluation at 09:15 IST.
